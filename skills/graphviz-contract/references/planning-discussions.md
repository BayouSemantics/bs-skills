# Planning Discussions

During planning, create DOT/PNG drafts in `<repo-root>/tmp/graphviz-contracts/`, labeled
"Proposed", and open them for discussion (see [Writing and Editing](writing-and-editing.md)).
Drafts need no router entries, metadata, or checkpoints. Preserve the current diagram's layout and
resolution where practical. Subdue unchanged context; mark changes **[ADD]**, **[REMOVE]** (also dashed),
or **[CHANGE]** with a brief explanation and legend. For extensive changes, show **Current** and **Proposed**
side by side. Explain the behavioral change, rationale, and decision needed.

Save approved planning graphs (both DOT and PNG) alongside the project's planning documentation
and reference them from the plan; use durable paths outside `tmp`. Keep them labeled "Proposed"
until implemented. After implementation, update maintained diagrams to match the code and follow
the checkpoint workflow.

During your discussions, refer to specific headings, flows, relationships, or any other relevant
annotations within the relevant diagram, if the details are helpful for your explanation.
